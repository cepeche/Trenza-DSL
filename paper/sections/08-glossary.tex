\section{Spanish Terminology Reference}
\label{sec:glossary}

The following table maps Spanish-language identifiers from the original
development artefacts to the English terms used in this paper and its figures.
These names appear verbatim in the repository's \texttt{.trz} source files and
generated outputs. They are preserved unchanged in the public repository as
evidence that the system was built for personal use---not for
publication---before Trenza existed as a language.
